**KitsuneOS** is a free Discord game: catch, refine and battle effigies of a server's
original characters. It is a hobby project operated by **Deaththegrim**.

These are its two policy documents.

## [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }})

What the game stores — Discord user and server IDs, display names and avatars used to build
the roster, artwork you upload, feedback you send — and what it deliberately does not: no
email, no payment details, no message content, no analytics or tracking. Also covers where
the data is held, your opt-in and opt-out controls, and how to have your data removed.

## [Terms of Service]({{ '/terms/' | relative_url }})

The rules of play. The game is free and always will be, in-game items have no monetary value
and may not be sold for real money, you keep ownership of characters and art you submit, and
effigies of real members are only catchable if that member opts in themselves. Also covers
moderation, the fact that the service can change or stop, and liability.

---

Questions, or a request to remove your data: use the in-game feedback command, or DM the
operator on Discord (**Deaththegrim**).
